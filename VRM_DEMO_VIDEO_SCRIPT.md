## TECHNICAL PRODUCTION NOTES

### Recording Setup:
**Screen Recording:**
- Resolution: 1920x1080 (1080p) minimum, 3840x2160 (4K) preferred
- Frame Rate: 60 FPS for smooth animation playback
- Software: OBS Studio or Camtasia
- Cursor: Large, visible custom cursor with click animations
- Hotkey: F9 to start/stop recording

**Audio:**
- Microphone: Professional USB condenser (Blue Yeti, Audio-Technica AT2020)
- Sample Rate: 48kHz, 16-bit minimum
- Noise Reduction: Apply in post-production
- Voiceover: Record separately from screen capture
- Background Music: Royalty-free from Epidemic Sound, Artlist, or Uppbeat

**Lighting:**
- If showing on-camera talent: 3-point lighting setup
- Screen brightness: Medium (avoid overexposure in recordings)

### Post-Production:
**Editing Software:**
- Adobe Premiere Pro, Final Cut Pro, or DaVinci Resolve

**Visual Effects:**
- Text overlays: After Effects or Premiere Pro
- Motion graphics: After Effects templates or custom animations
- Transitions: Smooth fades, slide-ins (avoid jarring cuts)
- Color grading: Consistent, modern look with slight saturation boost

**Audio:**
- EQ voice for clarity (boost 2-5kHz, reduce 200-400Hz mud)
- Compression: Gentle 3:1 ratio for consistent volume
- Background music: -18dB to -12dB (don't overpower voice)
- Sound effects: Button clicks, success chimes, whoosh transitions

**Captions:**
- Closed captions: Auto-generate via YouTube, then manually correct
- Burned-in captions: Large, readable font (Montserrat, Roboto)
- Position: Bottom-center or bottom-third
- Style: White text with black outline/shadow

### Export Settings:
**YouTube Upload:**
- Codec: H.264 (High Profile)
- Container: MP4
- Resolution: 1080p or 4K
- Frame Rate: 60 FPS
- Bitrate: 12-16 Mbps (1080p), 35-45 Mbps (4K)
- Audio: AAC, 192-320 kbps, 48kHz

**Social Media Versions:**
- Twitter: 1080p, 2:20 max length, square (1:1) or vertical (9:16)
- Instagram: 1080x1080 (square), 60 seconds max
- LinkedIn: 1080p landscape, 10 minutes max
- TikTok: 1080x1920 (vertical), 3 minutes max

### Thumbnail Design:
**Requirements:**
- Resolution: 1280x720 pixels
- Format: JPG or PNG
- File size: Under 2MB
- Safe area: Avoid text in corners (profile pics cover them)

**Thumbnail Concept:**
- Background: Split screen (manual vs AI)
- LEFT: Frustrated person with complex software UI
- RIGHT: Clean Hyperscape interface with VRM model
- Text overlay: "AI-Powered VRM Generation"
- Big text: "10 MINUTES" in bold, eye-catching font
- Face: Exaggerated expression (if including person)
- Colors: High contrast, saturated (blue/orange/red)
- Border: 2-3px white border for visibility in search
